About me

Welcome.

My name is Ngo Phuoc Nguyen Ngoc. I received my Ph.D. in Statistics from the University of São Paulo, Brazil, in 2022, under the supervision of Professors Vladimir Belitsky and Gunter M. Schütz. I am currently a lecturer and researcher at the Institute of Research and Development, Duy Tan University, Vietnam.

Research interests

My research interests are mainly in probability and statistical physics. I am particularly interested in interacting particle systems, random walks, and phase transitions.

Within interacting particle systems, I study exactly solvable models and their applications. I also work on random walks with memory, drift, or reinforcement, with particular interest in recurrence, transience, and scaling behavior. More broadly, I am interested in stochastic analysis and related problems in applied probability and statistics.

  • Interacting particle systems
  • Random walks
  • Phase transitions
  • Applied probability
